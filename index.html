The Aerospace Precedent

A Neural Network Must Never Hold
Unmediated Authority Over Actuators

In 1988, Airbus pioneered Flight Envelope Protection (FEP) for the A320. No matter how violently a pilot pulled the sidestick, the flight control computer projected the input onto the aerodynamic flight envelope: preventing aerodynamic stall, structural over-G, and loss of control.

Modern robotics is repeating commercial aviation’s early vulnerability: feeding raw 10–30 Hz outputs from Vision-Language-Action (VLA) diffusion policies (OpenVINO, ACT, SmolVLA) directly into low-level joint position servos. When grasping a shared rigid workpiece, microscopic independent joint coordinate drift creates massive internal stress.
